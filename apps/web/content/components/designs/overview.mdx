# Designs overview

Tawny designs are full-site templates you can preview in-product or open as edge-to-edge demos.

## Available designs

### Art

A bilingual bento journal with notes, projects, and location pages. Locale-aware routing (`en` / `zh`) and rich MDX shortcodes.

- Live showcase: [/designs/art](/designs/art)
- Full preview: [/view/art](/view/art)

### Dev

A mono developer blog with projects and technical posts. English-only, terminal-inspired chrome.

- Live showcase: [/designs/dev](/designs/dev)
- Full preview: [/view/dev](/view/dev)

### Link

A minimal personal link-in-bio page with profile, social links, and a short tagline.

- Live showcase: [/designs/link](/designs/link)
- Full preview: [/view/link](/view/link)

### VCard

A dark personal vcard with a profile sidebar, selected work, and short notes.

- Live showcase: [/designs/vcard](/designs/vcard)
- Full preview: [/view/vcard](/view/vcard)

### Ocean

A scroll-driven WebGL landing page. A particle whale, sea turtle, jellyfish, and manta ray — each sampled from a glTF model — morph into one another as you scroll.

- Live showcase: [/designs/ocean](/designs/ocean)
- Full preview: [/view/ocean](/view/ocean)

### Board

An issue dashboard. Sidebar, toolbar, and a 4px list: 16px icons on the text line, labels and ids on the left, numbers and dates on the right.

- Live showcase: [/designs/board](/designs/board)
- Full preview: [/view/board](/view/board)

### Mug

A ceramics studio for Jane Mug. The home, the sign-in, and the sales dashboard are one design.

- Live showcase: [/designs/mug](/designs/mug)
- Full preview: [/view/mug](/view/mug)

### Admin

The Jane Mug sales dashboard, listed on its own. The same screen is still inside Mug at `/admin`.

- Live showcase: [/designs/admin](/designs/admin)
- Full preview: [/view/admin](/view/admin)

### Desk

A project desk. Sidebar, stat row, weekly chart, team, projects, and a running timer.

- Live showcase: [/designs/desk](/designs/desk)
- Full preview: [/view/desk](/view/desk)

### Faculty

A USC faculty page. Charcoal page, light panel, cardinal banner, and a publications list with BibTeX.

- Live showcase: [/designs/faculty](/designs/faculty)
- Full preview: [/view/faculty](/view/faculty)

## Showcase vs preview

| Path | Chrome | Use when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/designs/<id>` | Tawny navbar + product frame | Comparing templates in context |
| `/view/<id>` | None | Reviewing the design as a standalone site |

<Callout type="note" title="Note">
  Scaffold any template with [create-tawny](/components/create-tawny) — the CLI copies from the matching `templates/` package.
</Callout>
