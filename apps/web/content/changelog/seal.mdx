Seal is a one-person editorial studio. Each chapter is a portrait poster: a short Traditional Chinese title set vertically, a mark beside it, and a horizontal note underneath. The titles stay vertical on a phone. The page is warm paper with a quiet grain, and the seal is a wax impression.

## Work

Open a piece from the home, or send a note. The confirmation stays in the browser.

```bash
npx create-tawny@latest seal my-seal
```
