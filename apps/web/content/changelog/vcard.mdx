VCard is a dark personal site. A sticky profile card sits beside an article, and the frosted nav opens About, Resume, Project, and Blog.

<ChangelogPreview id="vcard" />

## Open the pages

About keeps selected work, a see-more pill, and writing cards beside a tech-stack column. Resume is a timeline: a gold dot and a hairline connect each institution card. Project and Blog filter in place, and each card opens a detail page.

Scaffold a copy, then replace the name, contacts, and sample pages in `lib/profile.ts`.

```bash
npx create-tawny@latest vcard my-vcard
```

### Improvements

- Added the VCard template to `create-tawny`
- Added a live demo at `/designs/vcard` and a full preview at `/view/vcard`
- About, Resume, Project, and Blog share one article width, including each detail page
- Writing on the home page is gradient cards plus a tech-stack column
- Resume uses the dotted timeline list
- Testimonials, tilted photos, and lifestyle cards are in the component gallery
