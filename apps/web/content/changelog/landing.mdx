Landing is a small-batch ceramics home page. A round hero, three pieces from the latest firing, the four-step process, and a studio note introduce Jane.

<ChangelogPreview id="landing" />

## Open the studio

The bag starts with two sample pieces and stays in the browser. Sign-in stays on the page. The potter in the sample copy is Jane.

```bash
npx create-tawny@latest landing my-landing
```

### Improvements

- Added the Landing template to `create-tawny`
- Added a live demo at `/designs/landing` and a full preview at `/view/landing`
- The bag holds sample pieces without a shop backend
