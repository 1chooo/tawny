Login is the ceramics studio sign-in. The left half holds the firing photograph. The right half is the wordmark, email, and password.

<ChangelogPreview id="login" />

## Stay on the page

Submitting the form does not open a session. Add `?next=/admin` to switch the heading to the studio account. The sample studio is Jane Mug.

```bash
npx create-tawny@latest login my-login
```

### Improvements

- Added the Login template to `create-tawny`
- Added a live demo at `/designs/login` and a full preview at `/view/login`
- The form stays on the page until you connect your own auth
