Vale is a white storefront for a small ceramics and painting studio. Category names are large and condensed. Product photographs sit in a bordered grid. The cart, the mailing list, and checkout stay in the browser.

<ChangelogPreview id="vale" />

## Shop

Switch Ceramics, Paintings, and Others on the home. Open a piece, choose options when a work asks for them, and review the cart. Payment is not connected.

```bash
npx create-tawny@latest vale my-vale
```

### Improvements

- Added the Vale template to `create-tawny`
- Added a live demo at `/designs/vale` and a full preview at `/view/vale`
- The catalog, product pages, and cart share one browser cart
- Sold-out, paused, and option pieces follow the sample inventory
