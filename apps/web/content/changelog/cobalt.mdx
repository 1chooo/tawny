Cobalt is one ceramics studio. The home introduces Jane, the sign-in keeps the kiln photograph, and the sales board shows revenue, restock, and customers.

<ChangelogPreview id="cobalt" />

## Three pages

Sign in and Studio admin are pages in the same app. The bag stays in the browser. The dashboard numbers are sample data, and the charts stay still.

```bash
npx create-tawny@latest cobalt my-cobalt
```

### Improvements

- Added the Cobalt template to `create-tawny`
- Added a live demo at `/designs/cobalt` and a full preview at `/view/cobalt`
- Home, sign-in, and studio admin share one design
