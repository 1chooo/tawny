A route sheet in the same language as the issue list. One accent, a hairline border, and a 4px grid. The timeline follows a bike trip to Wall Street, and the same sheet carries two more ways to get there.

<ChangelogPreview id="route-sheet" />

## Three trips

Bike keeps the dock pickup on W 84 St and the dropoff at Barrow St. Drive follows the West Side Highway and parks at the Battery Parking Garage. Transit boards the 1 train at 86 St and gets off at Wall St.

The squiggle stays on the bike leg. A walk or a drive is a straight line, and the train is dashed.
